I am a GPU architect at Apple. Previously, I did my PhD at UC Santa Cruz, where I worked with [Tyler Sorensen](https://users.soe.ucsc.edu/~tsorensen/). My thesis work focused on GPU programming models, including designing and evaluating techniques to test the conformance of compilers and hardware to memory model specifications ([1](assets/pdf/mc_mutants.pdf), [2](assets/pdf/gpuharbor.pdf)), and testing and improving the safety properties of GPU programming languages in the face of data races ([3](assets/pdf/saferace.pdf)). I have also worked on bringing portable and private AI inference to the browser, building [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp)'s WebGPU backend from scratch, including many of the kernels and scheduling logic.
